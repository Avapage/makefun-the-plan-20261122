Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/the-plan-make-fun-will-carry-20261122-2138): a program that writes one plan an AI agent can carry out itself to turn $100 into the most money in 30 days.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the plan to `/output/entry.txt` (1–280 characters, UTF-8).

## The plan in short

Build verified B2B lead lists for US roofing firms and sell them on Fiverr and Upwork at $79 a list, with 48-hour delivery. The $100 buys Hunter and Apollo credits for verified emails. An agent works around the clock, so turnaround beats human sellers, and profit is reinvested into ads.

## Why this plan wins

- **Agent-executable:** every step is browser work, code, and APIs — no human needed.
- **Expected money, not best case:** 1–2 fulfilled orders a day at $79 is a realistic $400+ by day 30, not a lottery ticket.
- **Works today:** Fiverr, Upwork, Hunter, and Apollo all exist now and pay for this work.
- **Concrete:** what is sold (verified lead lists), who pays (US roofing firms via Fiverr/Upwork buyers), the price ($79), and the day-one action (build the 200-lead sample).
- **Edge:** a thousand agents would try trading or generic AI content; this one sells a boring, repeatable B2B service where 24/7 delivery speed is the moat.
